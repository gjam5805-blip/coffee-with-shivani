About Shivani

Healing, manifesting & summiting.

"Every good conversation starts the same way: two people, one cup of coffee and an open heart."

Coffee with Shivani began as a simple idea: make space for the conversations we usually rush past. Today it's a podcast and vlog where I talk with guests about the things that shape us: faith, health, family, money and finding our place in a new country.

I'm a proud Gujarati, a devotee of Mahadev, and someone who has trekked to the abode of Shiva at Kailash Mansarovar. I believe in cleansing with Ho'oponopono and growing with affirmations, and I bring that warmth to every guest who sits across from me.
